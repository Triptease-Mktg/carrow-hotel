About the hotel

A 1927 office building, rebuilt for staying in

The Carrow Hotel occupies the former Carrow Printing Company building on North Carrow Street, two blocks from the river and a six-minute walk from Millennium Park. The building kept its steel frame, its terrazzo lobby floor and its brass elevator doors. Everything else is new.

We opened in 2023 with 112 rooms across twelve floors, a co-working floor for guests and locals, a gym that never closes and a rooftop bar that fills up by six. Breakfast is served in the lobby restaurant from 6:30 am, and the coffee bar by the front door pours until 4 pm. The L stops at State and Lake, three minutes away, and O'Hare is forty minutes on the Blue Line.

Our direct rate is always our lowest rate. Book with us and you also get free late checkout when the room is free, a welcome drink on the roof and no resort fee, because we do not charge one.
